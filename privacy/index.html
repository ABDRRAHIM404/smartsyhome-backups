Privacy policy.

This policy covers SmartsyHome Backups, the owner-operated encrypted backup utility, and this public documentation website.

Effective and last updated: November 1, 2026

Current status: the backup integration is being configured for its owner. Scheduled backups and live Gmail failure notifications have not been activated. This documentation site does not connect visitors to Google Drive, accept files or run a backup.

1. Who operates this utility

SmartsyHome Backups is maintained by the SmartsyHome owner, using the GitHub account ABDRRAHIM404. It supports the owner’s website recovery needs; it is not a public backup service. Privacy questions can be sent to abdorrahimbounga1@gmail.com. No separate registered company is represented by this policy.

2. Google Drive access and its purpose

The prepared integration requests the Google Drive drive.file permission: access to files the app creates, or that the account holder specifically selects or authorizes for the app. It does not request unrestricted access to all Drive files. Supplying a folder ID alone does not grant access; the folder must also be accessible under the permission granted by Google.

After the owner authorizes it, the utility uses Drive access to upload encrypted backup archives, list its backup files, download them for integrity and recovery checks, and remove older app-managed copies under the retention policy. It also checks available storage. This involves backup filenames, file and folder identifiers, sizes, timestamps, storage-quota information, authorization credentials and the archive bytes.

The owner designates a private backup folder. The folder setting determines where the utility operates; it is not an additional Google-enforced permission boundary. The utility is not intended to read, change or delete unrelated Drive documents. No broader Drive permission will be introduced without explaining the change and obtaining the necessary authorization.

3. What the archives contain

Archives contain SmartsyHome’s website database content and authorized uploaded media. This can include published articles, unpublished product drafts, editorial sources, image-permission records, settings and website analytics records such as page paths, referral categories, timestamps and daily pseudonymous visitor identifiers. Unpublished content remains private inside the backup.

Portable content backups exclude active authentication sessions and login-attempt records. OAuth tokens, Cloudflare access credentials and the separate recovery key are not intentionally included in the content archive. The utility does not use Drive authorization to browse personal documents or read Gmail messages.

4. How information is used and shared

Google user data is used only to provide and maintain the owner-authorized backup, storage, retention and recovery functions described here. It is not sold, used for advertising, used to build visitor profiles or used to train AI models.

Necessary service providers are Google Drive for off-site archive storage, GitHub Actions and GitHub Secrets for the prepared private automation and credentials, and Cloudflare for the source website database/media and a supplemental private encrypted copy. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. No backup data is placed in this public repository or its website.

Access is limited to the operator, authorized private-project maintainers and the services needed to perform the job. Provider-required processing, security operations and legally required disclosures may also apply. The owner must deliberately authorize any additional sharing.

5. Storage, encryption and credentials

The prepared runner encrypts archives before uploading them to Google Drive, using AES-256 authenticated encryption through GnuPG. It checks uploaded data by downloading, decrypting and restoring a copy, then comparing recovered content and media integrity. The separate recovery key is protected as a GitHub Secret for automation and in a private owner-controlled recovery copy; it is not stored in the Drive backup folder or this public site.

Cloud and Google authorization credentials are stored in private local configuration or encrypted GitHub Secrets. Authorized automation receives the credentials and key only when needed. Temporary plaintext and restored data are necessary during collection and verification. The job removes its temporary files on normal completion; GitHub-hosted runners are ephemeral. Private local verification or recovery copies may remain until the operator removes them.

Encryption reduces exposure but does not protect against every risk, such as compromise of the authorized runner, recovery key or owner accounts. Recovery-key loss can make archives unrecoverable. Provider account controls and available storage still apply.

6. Retention, deletion and revocation

The prepared retention policy keeps the newest 14 app-managed encrypted archives in Google Drive and seven supplemental verified copies in private Cloudflare R2. Cleanup is limited to the utility’s recognized backup files and runs after required recovery verification succeeds. It does not delete unrelated Drive files. Local copies and older operational archives may require separate operator cleanup.

These limits apply to successful runs, not a guaranteed number of calendar days. Paused or failed jobs may leave older files longer. Drive deletions may place files in Google Drive’s trash; permanent removal and storage accounting follow Google’s settings and policies. Removing a record from the active website does not immediately remove it from every retained archive.

The account holder can revoke access through Google Account connections, disable the backup job and remove its credentials. Revoking access stops future authorized Drive operations but does not automatically delete existing archives. The owner can delete remaining Drive/R2/local copies or request assistance through the contact address below.

7. Gmail notifications and contact messages

When separately configured and enabled, backup failure alerts are sent to abdorrahimbounga1@gmail.com. The prepared sender uses Gmail SMTP with a dedicated sending credential. It does not request Gmail API access or read the mailbox. Alerts contain a minimal job stage and error category, not archive contents, secrets or provider error payloads.

If you email the contact address, Gmail processes your address, message and any attachments to deliver the correspondence. The operator uses that information to answer the request. Do not email passwords, tokens, recovery keys or unencrypted backup archives. Correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request and related obligations; it has no automatic backup-retention deadline.

8. Visitors to this public website

This static GitHub Pages site has no forms, account registration, embedded analytics, advertising scripts or app-set cookies. Visiting it does not authorize Google Drive access or expose the owner’s backups.

GitHub provides hosting and may process connection and access information, including IP addresses, for operating and securing its service. External links and email links take you to services with their own policies. See the GitHub Privacy Statement, Google Privacy Policy and Cloudflare Privacy Policy. Provider processing may occur in countries other than your own.

9. Questions, requests and policy changes

For access, correction, deletion or other privacy requests, contact abdorrahimbounga1@gmail.com. The operator may need to verify your authority before acting. Requests are handled according to the applicable circumstances and law; independent providers control some hosting and account records.
